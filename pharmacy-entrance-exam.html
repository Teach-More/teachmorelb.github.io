Course overview

Everything you need, in one focused programme

An intensive, exam-focused program built to get you into Pharmacy school. We turn the toughest topics into clear, memorable concepts, then lock them in with serious practice, so you walk into the entrance exam knowing exactly what to do. Structured lessons, real exam questions, and personal guidance from an experienced pharmacist and educator, all aimed at one goal: you, passing.

Who it's for

  • High school graduates applying to Pharmacy faculties
  • Students preparing for entrance exams in Lebanon
  • Anyone who needs focused practice and clear explanations

Content & structure

  • Intensive practice: 1000+ exam-style questions, past papers, and timed exercises
  • Mock exams: full-length practice tests under real exam conditions
  • Personalized support: direct Q&A and follow-up until full understanding

Key outcomes

  • Master all tested subjects with confidence
  • Develop strong exam strategies and time management
  • Practice enough to enter the exam fully prepared
  • Maximize your chances of university acceptance
